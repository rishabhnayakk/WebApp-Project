Frequently asked questions

Technical specifications, storage rules, and DOT UN1950 shipping regulations.

What propellant system do Aerosol Webapp aerosols use?

All Aerosol Webapp products utilize Eco-HFO 1234ze or purified nitrogen with GWP < 1, compliant with CARB 2026 and EU F-Gas regulations. No HFC-134a or ozone-depleting propellants are used.

Can the canisters spray fully inverted?

Yes. Formulations designated with 360° All-Angle technology feature dual-port dip tubes that provide uniform 12-micron atomization at any angle, including upside down.

Why is air freight shipping not available?

Under DOT 49 CFR and IATA DGR, pressurized canisters are classified UN1950 and must travel via certified ground HazMat carriers to ensure safety.

What storage temperatures are required?

Store all canisters between 0°C and 50°C (32°F to 122°F). Keep away from open flames, electrical arcs, and direct radiant heat.

Where can I download official Safety Data Sheets (SDS)?

Full GHS-compliant Safety Data Sheets are available on individual product detail pages and are automatically attached to order dispatch confirmations.
